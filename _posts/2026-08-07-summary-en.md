## [Chinese-led BESIII team confirms existence of glueballs after 15 years](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 6, the Institute of High Energy Physics (IHEP) announced that the BESIII collaboration, led by Chinese scientists, confirmed the existence of glueballs for the first time. The team identified the particle X(2370), first observed in 2011, as predominantly composed of gluons, matching glueball properties. This is the clearest experimental evidence for glueballs in nearly five decades, a milestone that tests the Standard Model of particle physics. Confirming glueballs validates a key prediction about the strong force and opens a new chapter in understanding the quantum chromodynamics sector. The BESIII detector at the Beijing Electron-Positron Collider (BEPCII) was used, and the X(2370) particle's quantum numbers were measured as 0−+ in 2024, consistent with a pseudoscalar glueball. New decay modes and flavor-singlet properties were further confirmed in the latest research.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 6, 07:31

**Background**: In particle physics, a glueball is a hypothetical composite particle consisting solely of gluons, which are the force carriers of the strong interaction. Because gluons carry color charge, they can bind to each other without any quarks, a phenomenon predicted by quantum chromodynamics but never previously observed experimentally. The BESIII experiment has been searching for glueball candidates in particle decays since its data collection began.

## [Round-Trip Consistency Gives Diffusion Models a Self-Supervised Error Signal](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vh2gn1/roundtrip_consistency_bidirectional_diffusion/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers introduced a bidirectional conditional latent diffusion model that steps dynamical systems forward or backward in time via a direction flag. Using round-trip consistency—rolling forward i steps and then backward i steps—the model produces a measurement-free, self-supervised test-time proxy for rollout error. This provides a way to estimate rollout error without ensembles, held-out data, or governing equations, which is especially valuable for real-world deployment where ground truth is unavailable. Training one bidirectional model also outperforms two specialist unidirectional models, suggesting efficiency and generalization gains for long-horizon prediction tasks. The paper is available at arXiv:2608.00675, with code on GitHub and a project page. The method was demonstrated on CELEBV-HQ video generation and turbulent plasma fields (digital twins).

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Clean-Hovercraft5825 · Aug 6, 12:10

**Background**: Autoregressive models such as latent diffusion or flow models generate sequences by repeatedly sampling the next step; however, small errors compound over long rollouts, and at deployment there is usually no ground truth to measure correctness. Round-trip consistency exploits the idea that if a model can run both forward and backward in time, returning to the starting point after a round trip measures its own error in a self-supervised manner. Similar bidirectional approaches have also been explored for magnetohydrodynamics and other dynamical systems.
